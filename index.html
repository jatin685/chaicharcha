Brand Overview

The Story Behind Chai & Charcha

Founded by Jatin, Chai & Charcha was created to revive India's beloved baithak culture — blending traditional street teas with artisanal modern dining.

🫖

Authentic Kulhad Teas

From slow-simmered ginger adrak masala chai to royal Kashmiri saffron kahwa, our teas are steeped using traditional whole spices in natural earthenware cups.

☕

Specialty Kaapi & Espresso

We source plantation Arabica beans from Chikmagalur and Kodagu, roasted to perfection for velvety flat whites, iced cold brews, and authentic South Indian degree filter kaapi.

🍕

Wood-Fired Gourmet Pizzas

48-hour fermented sourdough crusts baked at 450°C in our wood-fired oven. Topped with charred tandoori paneer tikka, fresh basil, and creamy buffalo mozzarella.

🍹

Fresh Botanical Mocktails

Handcrafted crushed-ice refreshments made with freshly picked garden mint, key lime juice, wild jamun berries, and fizzy sparkling soda to rejuvenate your day.
